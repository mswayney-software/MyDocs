# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Dark mode (View menu): Light, Dark, or Match my device. The page goes dark too, with text colors and highlights kept readable; printing and exports stay black on white.
- Typewriter light (View menu): a small LED just under the word being typed, its light fanning up over the word and the lines above, the rest of the page in shadow. It follows the word, glides from line to line and scrolls with the text.

## 1.0.0

First release. Everything in the v1 plan:

- Text and paragraph formatting, title and headings, lists, block quotes, links, page breaks, undo and redo, keyboard shortcuts
- Tables (rows, columns, merged cells, header rows) and images (insert, paste, drag, resize, align), stored inside the document
- Find and replace, word and character count, zoom
- Native `.owdoc` files: open, save, save as; Ctrl+S writes back to the file in Chrome and Edge, downloads a copy elsewhere
- Backups within a second of every change, crash recovery, the last 10 versions kept, unsaved-changes warning
- Works offline after the first visit; installable in Chrome and Edge; bundled fonts metric-compatible with Times New Roman, Arial and Calibri
- Page setup: Letter or A4, margins, header, footer, page numbers including MLA's last name and page number
- Print and save as PDF
- Word files: open .docx as a copy, save as .docx
- MLA paper and APA student paper templates
- Accessibility scan clean (WCAG 2.1 A and AA); typing stays responsive in a 50-page document on a slow machine
